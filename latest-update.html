Status Update & Meeting Report: October 2026

Our first community FIKA, growing international outreach, and the working groups that will carry the alliance through Phase 1.

1.Executive Summary & FIKA Community Meeting

  • Inaugural Community FIKA: Successfully hosted the first informal check-in meeting (termed the 'IPA FIKA') bringing together national representatives to establish community rapport, review alliance objectives, and align on initial governance.
  • Meeting Dynamics: Total duration of 65 minutes featuring round-the-table introductions, casual open sharing, and structured discussion on working groups and immediate priorities.

2.Expanded International Outreach

  • Germany: Connected with Ficho from PolyFest Berlin, who is assisting in identifying a representative and will connect with collectives during PolyFest Berlin.
  • Croatia: Connection initiated via Ficho (who is Croatian and knows a local group leader); following up soon.
  • Poland: Contacted PoliamoriaPL on Facebook; they are consulting members to confirm representation.
  • Spain: Málaga group representative was unable to attend the initial meeting as an observer and has been re-invited for the next meeting.
  • Japan: A connection through Bluesky is planned.

3.Phase 1 Working Group Definitions

To ensure focused execution and manage volunteer capacity, the alliance streamlined its working groups into three primary Phase 1 working groups (integrating Knowledge-Sharing into Operations & Tech):

  • Communications Working Group: Responsible for external messaging, social media presence, media response toolkits, and campaign coordination.
  • Operations, Tech & Knowledge-Sharing Working Group: Manages digital infrastructure (website, domain management), technical specifications, shared repositories, and centralising legal/advocacy resources.
  • Membership & Onboarding Working Group: Oversees the vetting and onboarding of new country representatives, outreach templates, directory maintenance, and WhatsApp community channel administration.

Legal & Policy and Research working groups will be activated in Phase 2.

4.Actionable Task List & Immediate Next Steps

  • Project Contact Directory: Collect dedicated project email addresses from all participants to streamline calendar scheduling, document access, and official notifications.
  • Outreach & Onboarding: Continue active engagement with groups in Germany, Croatia, Poland, and other prospective member nations.
  • Task Alignment: Organise and assign pending operational tasks to respective Phase 1 working groups.
