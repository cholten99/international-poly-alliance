International Poly Alliance

A cross-border alliance connecting national polyamory and ethical non-monogamy collectives — sharing resources, coordinating campaigns, and standing in solidarity so no local group has to work alone.

PolyPortugal, UK Polyamory and six other national collectives are building shared resources so no group has to start from zero.

Connect

Breaking isolation by linking national collectives worldwide into a supportive peer network.

Protect

Collaborative advocacy for legal recognition, anti-discrimination, and family rights.

Empower

Sharing knowledge, event playbooks, and organisational toolkits across borders.
